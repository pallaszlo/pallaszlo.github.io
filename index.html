About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the Sapientia Hungarian University of Transylvania, Faculty of Economics, Socio-Human Sciences and Engineering, Department of Economic Sciences in Miercurea Ciuc, Romania.

My research focuses on optimization, with emphasis on both global and local optimization techniques. In the field of global optimization, I have worked extensively with stochastic methods, evolutionary algorithms, and interval arithmetic-based approaches. I am the co-developer of the GLOBAL optimization method and the UNIRANDI local search algorithm. Recently, my research has expanded to econometric models and financial market applications, including portfolio optimization and asset allocation strategies.

Research Highlights

GLOBAL Optimization Method

A multi-start type stochastic method for bound-constrained global optimization problems. The method combines sampling, clustering, and local search to efficiently find global minima. Available in Fortran, C, MATLAB, and Java implementations with parallelization support.

SpringerBrief Book (2018) Evolutionary Computation (2012)

UNIRANDI Local Search

A stochastic local search algorithm that performs line searches along promising random directions. The improved version incorporates information from previous optimization stages, combining random directions with more promising search directions to accelerate convergence.

CEJOR (2017) IEEE SACI (2015)

Applications

My optimization methods have been successfully applied to various challenging problems across multiple disciplines:

  • Economics: Random coefficient logit demand models and market analysis
  • Finance: Portfolio optimization using covariance matrix estimators
  • Engineering: Combustion mechanism optimization and chemical kinetics
  • Networks: Wireless sensor network localization
