About

I am a researcher specializing in global optimization algorithms, with a particular focus on stochastic methods and local search techniques. My work includes the development and improvement of the GLOBAL optimization method and the UNIRANDI local search algorithm.

My research interests include:

  • Global optimization and stochastic methods
  • Multi-start algorithms and clustering techniques
  • Local search methods and derivative-free optimization
  • Applications in economics and industrial engineering

Research Highlights

GLOBAL Optimization Method

A multi-start type stochastic method for bound-constrained global optimization problems. The method combines sampling, clustering, and local search to efficiently find global minima.

UNIRANDI Local Search

A stochastic local search algorithm that performs line searches along promising random directions. The improved version incorporates information from previous optimization stages.

Applications

My optimization methods have been applied to various fields including:

  • Random coefficient logit demand models in economics
  • Combustion mechanism optimization
  • Portfolio optimization strategies
  • Wireless sensor network localization
